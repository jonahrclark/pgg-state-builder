Executive Briefing & Orientation

Building a State: Capacity and Autonomy

You are appointed Head of State of a newly sovereign country of 18 million citizens. Your mission is to survive for 150 months by balancing two central forces of governance:

1. State Capacity

How much the state can actually get done: collect taxes, enforce laws, and deliver services across the whole country.

Example: A high-capacity state builds roads everywhere, collects taxes from everyone, and gets disaster relief out within hours.

2. State Autonomy

How free the state is from pressure by interest groups, business owners, political factions, and its own military and police.

Example: A high-autonomy state can pass an environmental law even if factory owners lobby against it, or arrest a corrupt general.

If Capacity and Autonomy both reach 0%, the state fails at once.

Keep both levels above 50% to ensure your state remains strong.
